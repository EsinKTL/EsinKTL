## 🎓 Research Profile

I am a Computer Engineering student focused on the intersection of **systems engineering and machine learning**.

My work centers on understanding how **algorithmic design, system-level constraints, and statistical learning methods** interact in real computational environments.

Rather than treating software as isolated applications, I focus on:
- system behavior under constraints  
- optimization of computational resources  
- learning under imperfect or incomplete data  
- bridging low-level execution with high-level inference  

---

## 🔬 Selected Technical Work (Research-Oriented Summary)

### 📊 Optimization in Machine Learning Systems
Developed a custom implementation of **L1-regularized logistic regression using FISTA**, focusing on:

- convergence behavior under sparsity constraints  
- regularization impact on generalization  
- comparison with standard solvers  

Extended experiments include:
- MCAR / MAR / MNAR missing label mechanisms  
- robustness under incomplete supervision  
- failure analysis of EM under label-dependent bias  

---

### ⚙️ Systems-Level Scheduling (Phoenix-RTOS)
Extended a microkernel scheduler with:

- group-based CPU allocation policies  
- deterministic quanta distribution across task groups  
- kernel-level modification of scheduling logic  

Explored:
- fairness vs efficiency trade-offs  
- hierarchical scheduling design  
- system-level resource control mechanisms  

---

### 🧵 Concurrency & Synchronization Models
Designed a multi-threaded FIFO system using:

- strict state-dependent constraints  
- semaphore-based synchronization only  
- “Passing the Baton” pattern for correctness guarantees  

Focus:
- elimination of busy waiting  
- correctness under concurrency constraints  
- liveness guarantees in producer-consumer systems  

---

### 🧠 NLP & Structured Data Systems
Built a pipeline for **hierarchical address parsing and normalization**, involving:

- TF-IDF vectorization  
- nearest neighbor retrieval  
- fuzzy matching for noisy real-world data  
- hierarchical entity extraction  

Focus:
- structured prediction from unstructured text  
- scalability on large datasets  
- robustness under noisy inputs

## 🧪 Methodological Approach

Across all projects, my workflow follows:

1. Formalize the problem as a computational model  
2. Identify constraints (data, system, performance)  
3. Design or adapt algorithms under those constraints  
4. Evaluate empirical behavior under controlled settings  
5. Analyze failure modes and boundary conditions
